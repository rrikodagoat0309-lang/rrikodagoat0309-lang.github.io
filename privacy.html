Riko Hermes — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 6, 2026

1. What this app does

Riko Hermes is a personal AI assistant application that accesses Google services on behalf of the account owner (rrikodagoat0309@gmail.com) to: read email for school and business monitoring, create and edit Google Docs (e.g. an academic project logbook), and manage files created by the app.

2. Data accessed

  • Gmail: reads emails in the authorized inbox (gmail.readonly). The app does not send email.
  • Google Docs: creates and edits documents on the owner's account (documents scope).
  • Google Drive: accesses only files that the app itself created or opened (drive.file). The app cannot see other Drive files.

3. How data is used

Data is used solely to operate the assistant: summarizing incoming messages, drafting replies for the owner to review, and maintaining the FYT0008 academic logbook. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

4. Where data is stored

Data processed by the app is stored on the account owner's personal computer in Hong Kong. It is never uploaded to third-party servers beyond Google's own APIs.

5. Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact: rrikodagoat0309@gmail.com
